Reach your landlord through the tenant dashboard

The tenant dashboard at bowlinglegacy.com is the most effective way to send maintenance requests, a 30-day move-out notice, payment arrangements and other documents to the landlord inbox. Download a fillable PDF, save your answers and attach it to a dashboard message. Review replies in My Requests.

For formal notices, also use the delivery method and notice period required by your lease and Oregon law. A dashboard message alone does not establish legal service. First-class mail generally adds three days; termination by email requires a qualifying written addendum and first-class mail.

Pay rent on the site or through Cash App $Trmf8763. Your bank's online bill pay can pay our business account; request verified instructions privately through the dashboard. Include your name, room and rent month, and allow time for receipt.

Agreed late fees are added automatically to the balance the day after the applicable legal and lease pay-by period expires, and remain separate from rent. Before the deadline, tell the landlord when you will pay and obtain written approval. Sending a request alone does not pause a fee. Existing signed lease terms control.
